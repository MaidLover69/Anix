# Code of Conduct

Anix is a small project made by volunteers. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests,
discussions or anywhere else the project lives) should be able to do so without harassment.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Linux, to programming or to English.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Explain your reasons and accept that the maintainer decides.
- Stay on topic. Issues and pull requests are for Anix itself.
- Respect people's privacy. Do not post someone else's personal details, accounts or messages.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, slurs, threats, or jokes at someone's expense.
- Sexual language or images, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Harassment, including following someone around the project or other sites because of it.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or repeated off-topic posts.
- Requests or code to break other services' protections, attack sites, or spread malware.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, report it privately. Do not argue in public.

- **On GitHub:** open a private report with the [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/MaidLover69/Anix/security/advisories/new)
  form and start the title with **Conduct:**. Only the maintainer can read it.
- **Anywhere:** use the platform's own **Report** option on the comment or account (GitHub and
  Codeberg both have one).

Include links to the comments involved and what happened. Reports are kept private, and the
person who reported is not named to the person reported.

## What happens next

Depending on how serious it is, the maintainer may:

1. Ask the person to stop, privately.
2. Hide or delete comments, lock a thread, or close an issue or pull request.
3. Block the person from the project, for a while or for good.

Severe cases (threats, doxxing, sexual harassment) can lead straight to a block.
